# Spectra Paints SQL Marketing Analysis

## Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing sales, customer and campaign data of a paint company using SQL to support marketing decisions on consumer growth, campaign effectiveness and product co-purchase patterns.

## Tools & Technologies

- MySQL
- SQL (Joins, Subqueries, CTEs)
- Window Functions (ROW_NUMBER, PERCENT_RANK, DENSE_RANK)
- CASE Statements and Views
- String, Math and Date-Time Functions

## Key Activities

- Understanding the six database tables (customers, items, transactions, coupons, cities, campaigns)
- Filtering and aggregating transactions by year, quarter, month, category, order type and city tier
- Measuring year-on-year sales growth and customer acquisition
- Analyzing campaign coupon impact on transactions and first purchases
- Segmenting household customers into income classes using percent rank
- Identifying products bought together by the same customers
- Deriving insights and campaign recommendations

## Key Insights

- Sales peaked in 2020 and declined in 2021 (-11%) and 2022 (-31%)
- Q4 is the busiest quarter with 130 of 300 transactions
- Oil Paint and Anti-Corrosive Paint contribute about 57% of sales
- Tier 2 cities generate about 62% of sales
- 67% of customers (89 of 133) made their first purchase with a campaign coupon
- Recommendation: run re-engagement coupon campaigns ahead of Q4 and target Tier 2 and Tier 3 cities

## Objective

To analyze the available sales, customer and campaign data using SQL and derive insights that help the marketing team plan campaigns and drive growth.

## About

SQL-based marketing and sales analysis for a paint company using MySQL, joins, window functions and CASE statements to uncover sales trends, campaign impact and customer behavior.
